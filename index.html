Song Guo (Chinese: 郭嵩) is a computer scientist known for fundamental and pioneering contributions to physical AI and edge intelligence.[1] He is a chair professor at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, with appointments in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, the Department of Electronic and Computer Engineering, and the Division of Integrative Systems and Design.[2] He also serves as director of the Center of Physical AI.

Education

Guo received his bachelor's degree in computer engineering from Huazhong University of Science and Technology in 1995, followed by a master's degree in the same field from Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications in 1998. He earned his PhD in computer science from the University of Ottawa in 2005 and subsequently held an NSERC postdoctoral fellowship at the University of British Columbia in 2006.

Career

Since joining the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology in 2023, Guo has led research in physical AI, including intelligent robotic systems, embodied AI, and foundation models for physical-world interaction. His team has developed approaches combining embodied reasoning, predictive world models, and robotic policy learning, while exploring how robots can improve their skills and world representations through feedback.[1][12][13] In edge intelligence, his work addresses distributed machine-learning training and inference, including edge-native foundation-model services across heterogeneous, resource-constrained devices.[14][15]

Guo has held editorial leadership positions in IEEE journals. He was the inaugural editor-in-chief of the IEEE Open Journal of the Computer Society (2020–2024).[16][17] He is editor-in-chief of IEEE Transactions on Cloud Computing[18] (2025–2028). Guo has also collaborated with industry partners on the development and application of physical AI and intelligent computing technologies.[19]
